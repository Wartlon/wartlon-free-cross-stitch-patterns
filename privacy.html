Privacy & Analytics

Wartlon uses Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use this website, such as which free patterns and guides are viewed, which PDF downloads are popular, and whether visitors follow links to the Wartlon Etsy shop.

Analytics is optional

Wartlon uses regional consent settings. For visitors in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom and Switzerland, analytics storage is denied by default until consent is granted. For visitors in other regions, analytics is enabled by default. Advertising storage and ad personalization are disabled everywhere.

What we measure

  • Page views and general site usage
  • Free cross stitch PDF download clicks
  • Clicks from this website to the Wartlon Etsy shop
  • Use of the cross stitch fabric size calculator
