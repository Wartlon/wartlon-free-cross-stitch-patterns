What Does 14 Count Aida Mean?

If a pattern recommends 14-count Aida, the number tells you the density of the fabric: there are 14 cross stitch squares in one inch.

Published August 31, 2026

14-count Aida explained

14-count Aida has 14 stitchable squares per inch. When you stitch one full cross over each Aida square, 14 crosses measure approximately one inch across. That is roughly 5.5 stitches per centimeter.

Finished size in inches = number of stitches ÷ 14

How big will my design be?

A 70-stitch-wide design is about 5 inches wide on 14-count Aida. A 140-stitch-wide design is about 10 inches wide. This is the stitched area only; the fabric cut should include extra margin.

What does 14-count Aida look like?

Aida is woven into clearly defined blocks with visible holes at the corners. On 14 count, the squares are easy to identify while still producing a detailed result, which is why it is such a common counted cross stitch fabric.

14 count vs 11, 16 and 18 count

The pattern itself does not change: only the physical size of each stitch changes.

Is 14-count Aida good for beginners?

It is a popular beginner choice because its holes and squares are easy to identify and it suits a wide range of designs. If you want larger squares, 11 count may be easier to see; 16 or 18 count gives a finer result.

How much fabric should I buy?

Divide the chart width and height by 14, then add blank fabric around all four sides. Around 2–3 inches (5–7.5 cm) per side is a useful general starting point, though finishing methods vary.

Example: a 56 × 70 stitch design on 14-count Aida is approximately 4 × 5 inches before adding margin.

Quick FAQ

How many stitches are in one inch?

Fourteen full crosses fit into one inch when each cross covers one Aida square.

Is 14 count bigger than 18 count?

The individual squares are larger, so the same chart produces a larger finished design on 14 count.

Is 28-count evenweave the same size?

When stitched over two threads, 28-count evenweave has an effective count of 14 stitches per inch and gives approximately the same finished size.
